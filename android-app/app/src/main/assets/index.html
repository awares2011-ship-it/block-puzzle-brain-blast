Tips, modes & FAQ⌄

How to Play Block Puzzle: Brain Blast

  1. Drag a block from the tray at the bottom onto the 8×8 board.
  2. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score points.
  3. Clear several lines with one block for a big bonus, and keep clearing lines move after move to build a Combo multiplier.
  4. You get three blocks at a time. Blocks can't be rotated, so plan where each one will fit.
  5. The game ends when none of your remaining blocks fits on the board. Beat your best score and try again!

Game Modes

Classic

The endless, relaxing block puzzle. There is no timer – take your time, chase your high score and pick up right where you left off.

Adventure

Level after level of jewel puzzles. Clear lines that contain rubies, sapphires and emeralds to collect them and complete each level's goal.

Daily Challenge

A new board every day. Reach the target score to keep your daily streak alive.

Tips & Strategy

  • Keep the centre of the board open and build along the edges.
  • Always leave room for the big 3×3 square and the long 1×5 bar – they are the hardest blocks to fit.
  • Set up two lines at once instead of clearing them one by one – multi-line clears score far more.
  • Clear at least one line every three moves to keep your combo going.
  • Stuck? Tap the 💡 hint, or use Undo, Shuffle and Bomb power-ups. You earn more by clearing lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Block Puzzle: Brain Blast free?

Yes. Block Puzzle: Brain Blast is completely free to play, with no sign-up and no account needed.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. You can also add it to your home screen or install the Android app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the game keeps working without an internet connection, and the Android app can be played offline too.

How is the score calculated?

Every block cell you place earns 1 point. Clearing lines earns a bonus that grows with the number of lines cleared at once and is multiplied by your combo. Clearing the entire board adds a 300 point bonus.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your best score, current game, adventure level and daily streak are saved automatically on your device.
