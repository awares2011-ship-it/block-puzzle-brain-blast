Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle: Brain Blast · Last updated: October 2026

Block Puzzle: Brain Blast ("the game") is a free puzzle game for the web and Android. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own analytics or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, saved game, adventure level, daily streak, awards and settings are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's or site's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app is free because it shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic information.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

Website

  • The website shows no ads.
  • It loads the free “Fredoka” font from Google Fonts, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • A service worker caches the game's own files so it can work offline.

Android permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibrate – optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID – used by AdMob as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
